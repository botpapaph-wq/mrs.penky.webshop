Information

Frequently asked questions.

Ordering

Do I need an account to order?
No. You can check out as a guest.

Is there a minimum order?
Yes, ₱500. Postage costs almost the same for one item as for four, so a single small piece would cost more to send than it is worth. Two or three pieces together carry their shipping easily — which is also why our sets are better value than buying one at a time.

Which payment methods can I use?
GCash, Maya, QR Ph and credit or debit cards through PayMongo, and international payments through PayPal. All prices are shown and charged in Philippine Peso (₱). If you pay with a card issued abroad, your bank converts the amount at its own rate.

Is my payment secure?
Yes. Payments are processed by PayMongo and PayPal. Card details are entered on their systems and are never stored on ours.

Can I change or cancel an order?
Write to us as soon as possible. If the order has not yet been dispatched we can usually still change or cancel it.

Shipping

How much is shipping?
It is calculated at checkout from your address and cart, and shown as its own line before you pay. Free on orders of ₱800 or more. See Shipping.

How long will delivery take?
The estimate for your order appears at checkout. It varies by item and destination.

Do you ship outside the Philippines?
Yes, to selected countries in Southeast Asia. Import duties in the destination country are not included in the checkout total.

Will I get tracking?
Yes, by email as soon as the parcel is with the courier.

Products

Are the items handmade?
No. We select our products from suppliers rather than manufacturing them ourselves. We describe them as accurately as we can, and colour or finish may vary slightly from the photos.

Are the items blessed?
We do not make that claim. Our products are devotional in purpose; any blessing is a matter for you and your parish.

Returns

My item arrived damaged or wrong. What now?
Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. Replacement, exchange or refund follows — see the Refund Policy.

Can I return something I simply do not want?
Generally no, because the items are devotional or personal in nature. Exceptions are stated on the product page.

How long does a refund take?
Typically 5–10 business days after approval, depending on your payment provider.

Still stuck?

Write to us — we generally reply within 2–3 business days.
